package.json

"express": "^5.2.1" ^ represents 5.X.X Minor and Patch will be auto updated.
"express": "~5.2.1" ~ represents 5.2.X Only Patch can be updated.

package.lock.json

as we saw above , versions can auto upgrade as per our requirement henceforth take a look into package.lock.json to find the exact version.

"express": "5.2.1" : exact version
